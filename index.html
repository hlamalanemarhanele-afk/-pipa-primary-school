Our Story — Only Pipa Original

1938: Mr Sesweni started Sub A & Sub B.
1945: School closed — parents said herd cows.
1965: Reopened — Mr Mancha Mochike led till 1982.
1983-2005: Mr Motshekga.
2006: Mr Machipi — went to PEU 2010 — Mr Makgalo acted till 2024.
2024-2025: Mrs Mohale + Mr Mabotja acted.
8 April 2025: Dr Matshipi M.G — came with 33 teachers, now 40, 1491 learners.
