📚 Historical Background

Established 1938 as off-shoot of Medingeni Primary, Sub A & B under Mr Sesweni. Closed 1945 because children forced to herd livestock. Reopened & relocated 1965 under Mr Mancha Stephen Mochike until 1982. Mr Makgwana Lazarus Motshekga 1983-2005 (retired). Mr Mothoka Ben Machipi 2006, seconded to PEU 2010. Mr Seja Stan Makgalo acting until retirement 2024, assisted by Mr Mabotja P.G. Mrs Matsatsi Jelos Mohale acting until March 2025 retirement. Dr Matshipi M.G took over 8 April 2025 to date, filled vacancies from 33 to 40 + Principal = 41 total.

🎯 Vision

To provide quality and innovative education programmes for learners across all grades, ensuring education is delivered with excellence, professionalism, innovation, and integrity.

🚀 Mission

Provide quality learning, improve literacy and numeracy outcome, promote discipline and good behaviour, assess, monitor learners progress and develop them holistically to be better citizen, create safe and inclusive environment.

💎 Values

Professionalism, transparency, honesty, discipline, respect, excellency, fairness and justice.
